# Temporary Power Architecture Sources

These sources support the planning logic used by the Temporary Power workflow. The application produces a customer-conversation aid and conceptual planning package, not a sealed electrical design.

## Generator paralleling

- Caterpillar, Generator Set Paralleling: paralleling supports redundancy, expansion, and lower ownership cost.
  - https://www.cat.com/en_GB/by-industry/electric-power/electric-power-products/custom-power-solutions/ep-custom-products/custom-control-systems-and-switchgear/generator-controllers/generator-set-paralleling.html
- Caterpillar, Specifying Paralleled Generation Systems: multiple units can increase reliability, support load prioritization, improve operating-load posture, and simplify maintenance and expansion.
  - https://www.cat.com/en_GB/by-industry/electric-power/Articles/ep-news/ep-news-specifying-paralleled-generation-systems.html
- Caterpillar, Distributed Synchronization and Paralleling Control: N+1 means the units required for the load plus one additional unit, with project-specific controls needed to avoid single points of failure.
  - https://www.cat.com/en_US/by-industry/electric-power/Articles/White-papers/distributed-synchronization-and-paralleling-control.html

## Temporary transformers

- United Rentals transformer catalog: published rental classes include 15, 30, 45, 75, 150, 300, 375, and 500 kVA temporary transformers, including 480 V to 208/120 V configurations.
  - https://www.unitedrentals.com/marketplace/equipment/power-generation-equipment/transformers
- Sunbelt Rentals 45 kVA 480 V to 208 V transformer: example of a temporary three-phase step-down transformer feeding portable distribution.
  - https://www.sunbeltrentals.com/equipment-rental/generators-and-accessories/45-kva-480v-208v-transformer/0060810/

## Guardrails

- Cooling tons are thermal capacity and are not converted directly to generator electrical kW. The Temporary Power workflow accepts the selected cooling equipment's electrical demand as a separate input.
- Generator, transformer, switchgear, cable, protection, grounding, fault-current, selective-coordination, environmental, sound, fuel, access, and clearance decisions require vendor data and qualified engineering review.
- Equipment footprints are conceptual planning envelopes for comparison. Vendor submittals and the verified site layout control final placement.
